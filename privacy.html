Privacy policy – rclone-astro

rclone-astro is used only by its owner. It uses the Google Drive API (scope drive.file) to upload and download the owner's own astrophotography files with rclone.

No data is collected, stored, shared or sold by this app or by anyone else. Files move only between the owner's own computers and the owner's own Google Drive. Access tokens stay on the owner's own computers.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
